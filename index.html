FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our programmes, entry requirements, learning formats and enrolment.

What programmes does the Centre offer?

The Centre focuses on two professional development programmes: the Cambridge International Certificate in Teaching & Learning for teachers, and Best Practice Network’s National Professional Qualifications (NPQs) for educational leaders.

Who is the Cambridge programme for?

The Cambridge Certificate in Teaching & Learning (Syllabus 5881) is for Vietnamese teachers who teach their subjects in English (EMI). Teachers develop their teaching skills through classroom practice, guidance from a professional mentor and building a portfolio.

Does Sedbergh Vietnam deliver the NPQs?

Sedbergh Vietnam is an Accredited Delivery Partner of Best Practice Network (BPN). The NPQs are accredited by England’s Department for Education (DfE).

How are the programmes delivered?

Programmes combine online learning with classroom practice, professional mentoring sessions, workshops and opportunities to share experience with colleagues. Learning focuses on practical application and evidence-based professional growth.

How do I apply?

Leave your details through the consultation form. Our team will share programme details, intake dates, fees and how to register. Submitting the form does not confirm enrolment or create any payment obligation.
