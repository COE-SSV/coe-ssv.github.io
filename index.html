FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our programmes, entry requirements, learning formats and enrolment.

What programmes does the Centre offer?

The Centre focuses on two professional development programmes: the Cambridge International Certificate in Teaching & Learning for teachers, and Best Practice Network’s National Professional Qualifications (NPQs) for educational leaders.

What is an NPQ and how is it recognised?

An NPQ (National Professional Qualification) is a qualification for developing specialist and leadership skills in education, accredited by England’s Department for Education (DfE). Sedbergh Vietnam is an accredited partner of Best Practice Network (BPN) to deliver these programmes.

What is the difference between the Cambridge programme and NPQs?

The Cambridge programme focuses on classroom teaching: over four months, teachers put new approaches into practice with a trained mentor, and Cambridge assesses their portfolio. NPQs focus on leadership, with seven pathways lasting 12–18 months, from specialist leadership to leading a whole school.

Who is eligible to join the programmes?

The Cambridge programme is for Vietnamese teachers who teach subjects in English (EMI). Because teachers apply new approaches in their own classrooms, you need to be teaching throughout the programme. NPQs are for both current and aspiring leaders, from subject and middle leaders to senior leaders, headteachers and executive leaders. Our team will confirm the specific entry requirements for each programme.

Who awards the qualifications?

The Cambridge International Certificate in Teaching & Learning is awarded by Cambridge International Education and is recognised at Level 4 on the UK Framework for Higher Education Qualifications (FHEQ). NPQs are awarded by Best Practice Network and accredited by England’s Department for Education (DfE).

How are the programmes delivered?

Programmes combine online learning with classroom practice, professional mentoring sessions, workshops and opportunities to share experience with colleagues. Learning focuses on practical application and evidence-based professional growth.

How much do the programmes cost, and when do they start?

NPQ fees range from £1,375 to £5,950 depending on the pathway, excluding an 11% administration fee. The next intake starts in January 2027, and the application deadline is Sunday 22 November 2026. Fees and dates for the Cambridge programme are being finalised; send an enquiry and we will share them as soon as they are confirmed.

How do I apply?

Leave your details through the consultation form. Our team will share programme details, intake dates, fees and how to register. Submitting the form does not confirm enrolment or create any payment obligation.
